## Purpose

Make Lighthouse Control Center recognizable across application and repository surfaces while preserving accessible presentation and explicit owner control over visual publication.

## ADDED Requirements

<!-- pdac-scope: cited -->

### Requirement: Recognizable Lighthouse identity

New identity assets SHALL use the canonical Lighthouse mark without altered geometry or proportions and the name Lighthouse Control Center, without software-factory wording or personal-owner framing. Asset provenance MUST identify the upstream revision.

#### Scenario: Mark and wordmark inspection

- **WHEN** a reviewer opens light and dark identity exports
- **THEN** the mark retains canonical geometry, the wordmark names Lighthouse Control Center and provenance identifies the upstream source and revision

### Requirement: Coastal bench banner

The banner candidate SHALL retain the selected source's bay, sea, lighthouse, boats and coastal viewpoint with a wooden slatted bench facing the water instead of a sofa. The supplied Loira photograph MUST inform final bench acceptance.

#### Scenario: Candidate composition review

- **WHEN** the maintainer compares the candidate, selected original and Loira reference
- **THEN** the coastal composition and bench direction are reviewable and any unavailable reference or unaccepted likeness remains explicit

### Requirement: Practical accessible identity delivery

The identity set SHALL include editable vector sources, transparent raster marks, favicon, touch/app icons and repository/social previews with documented dimensions and export instructions. Shell presentation MUST reuse Lighthouse themes, preserve keyboard focus, responsive layout and text status labels.

#### Scenario: Export and shell rehearsal

- **WHEN** exports are regenerated and the shell is inspected at desktop and narrow sizes in supported themes
- **THEN** dimensions match the inventory, banner/social content remains crop-safe, controls retain visible keyboard focus and status is understandable without color

### Requirement: Owner-controlled visual publication

Original sources and candidates SHALL remain intact. Selected published assets MUST NOT be replaced before rendered previews receive explicit maintainer visual acceptance.

#### Scenario: Review remains pending

- **WHEN** candidate assets exist but visual acceptance is not recorded
- **THEN** active asset references remain unchanged and delivery reports the required owner review rather than claiming publication or completion
