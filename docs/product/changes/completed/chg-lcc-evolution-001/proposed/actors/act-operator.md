## Purpose

An admitted individual or team member uses Lighthouse Control Center to understand and operate the projects within their granted scope.

## Goals

Find missions, inspect engineering health and follow authorized repository or infrastructure actions to their confirmed outcomes.

## Responsibilities

Inspect evidence, configure permitted project scope and request actions within explicit grants. Product decisions, architecture acceptance, integration and runtime administration remain separately authorized.

## Boundaries

A single-user deployment may admit one operator. Team deployments admit identities with explicit project and provider scope. Operator is an application role; repository ownership, product authority and runtime administration require their own grants. No person's name or personal-deployment instance defines this role.
