## Definition

A provider-scoped grouping of execution resources with declared membership, capacity evidence and supported administrative operations.

## Distinguish From

Pool capacity is not the number of registered runners or a guarantee that a queued job can execute.

## Usage

Inventory and capacity views retain the provider identity, policy scope and unknown measurements.
