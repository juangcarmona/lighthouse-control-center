## Definition

A measured or derived value with an identified purpose, source records, formula or source verdict, version, unit, time window, sample coverage, timestamps and limitations.

## Distinguish From

A measurement is distinct from a policy threshold, a composite score, a workflow result or a delivery acceptance.

## Usage

Health views retain unavailable and non-applicable values and expose the inputs to comparisons.
